About the league

A neighbourhood tournament with IPL theatre: auction drama, tennis-ball cricket, local pride.

Our story

Best cricket mornings in Howrah

Rankal Premiere League is in Season 8. Half-hand auction. Headly ball. Six and four every side. Players walk in as batsmen, bowlers or all-rounders. Franchises buy 11-a-side squads on 10 January.

Monu and Alamgir run the desk. Tournament nights are 16 and 17 January at Dakshin Howrah Milan Samity. Top four play IPL-style knockouts for the cup.

Format

How RPL is played

1

Registration

Players ₹200. Franchises ₹2000. UPI, bank or cash at the ground desk.

2

Auction

10 January. 5,000-point purse. Icon, captain, nine more names. Roster freeze that night.

3

League + playoffs

16 and 17 January. 5 overs a side. Top 4 into Qualifier 1, Eliminator, Qualifier 2, Final.

Auction culture

The hammer is part of the cricket

RPL is not just two days of batting. Captains learn the purse, protect base prices, and still leave room for an all-rounder at the death of the auction.
