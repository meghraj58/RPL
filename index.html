Tournament format

Points, playoffs, and the cup

Season 8 uses the playoff shape of a franchise T20 league: a points table, then four teams left. The cricket itself stays Rankal — five overs, headly ball, Dakshin Howrah Milan Samity.

2

Points

A win is worth 2 points. If a match cannot be finished, each side takes 1. Teams level on points are separated by net run rate.

4

Top four

First plays second in Qualifier 1. Third plays fourth in the Eliminator. The Qualifier 2 winner meets the Qualifier 1 winner in the Final.

11

Playing XI

Eleven a side. One Icon in the squad. Bowling limit 2+1+1+1. The umpire’s decision is final on the field.

★

Season honours

Man of the Match each game. Across the tournament: most runs, most wickets, best keeper, best fielder, fair play, and Man of the Tournament.

Player auction

Rules & guidelines

10 January. Every franchise builds exactly 11. Purse is 5,000 points. Bids do not come back.

01

Squad size

  • Exactly 11 players.
  • 1 Captain + 1 Icon + 9 players.
  • All 11 slots filled by the end of the auction.
02

Budget & purse

  • Total purse: 5,000 points.
  • Pre-draft Icon costs 1,500 points.
  • Starting budget 3,500 if Icon is picked early, else 5,000.
03

Squad needs

  • Categories: Batsman, Bowler, All-rounder.
  • Must buy at least 1 of each.
  • Remaining slots are free choice.
04

Base prices

  • Icon 300 base, +100 each bid.
  • All-rounder 150 base, +50 bid.
  • Batsman / Bowler 100 base, +50 bid.
05

Bidding

  • Increments +50 pts, +100 for Icons.
  • No retraction once a bid is placed.
  • Keep enough purse to fill 11 at base prices.
06

Unsold & lock

  • Unsold players return at the end.
  • Bidding stops at 11 slots.
  • Roster freeze is 100% final. No swaps without committee.
