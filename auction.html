Player auction rules

Guidelines for captains, owners and the Icon table. Play hard. Play fair. Fill eleven.

How the room works

5,000 points. 11 shirts. One hammer.

Every franchise starts with a 5,000-point purse. Pre-draft an Icon for 1,500 and you walk in with 3,500. Buy the Icon in the room and you keep the full 5,000 until the first strike.

Composition is locked: 1 Captain, 1 Icon, 9 players. Mandatory buys are one batsman, one bowler and one all-rounder. The rest of the XI is yours.

Guidelines

Six laws of the RPL auction

01

Squad size & composition

  • Total strength: exactly 11 players.
  • Shape: 1 Captain + 1 Icon + 9 players.
  • Full roster: every slot filled before the auction closes.
02

Budget & purse

  • Purse: 5,000 points per team.
  • Pre-auction Icon: 1,500 points.
  • Start: 3,500 if the Icon is already picked, 5,000 if bought live.
03

Squad requirements

  • Categories: Batsman, Bowler, All-rounder.
  • Mandatory: at least one of each.
  • Flex slots: remaining names are free choice.
04

Base prices & slabs

Icon Player300 +100
All-rounder150 +50
Batsman100 +50
Bowler100 +50
05

Bidding protocols

  • Increments +50 points, +100 for Icons.
  • No retraction. A bid stays on the board.
  • Reserve purse to complete 11 at base prices.
06

Unsold & final lock

  • Unsold names return in a last round.
  • Stop bidding when 11 slots are full.
  • Roster freeze is final. No swaps without the committee.
