Two nights. One champion.

Auction & matches

Hammer on 10 January. Cricket on 16 and 17 January. Top four into IPL-style playoffs.

01

Auction

10 January. Franchises spend 5,000 points on 11-player squads. Entry ₹2000 per franchise, ₹200 per player.

02

Tournament

16 and 17 January. 5 overs a side, headly ball, six and four every side.

03

Playoffs

Top 4. Qualifier 1, Eliminator, Qualifier 2, then the Final for the Season 8 cup.

Qualifier 1

1st vs 2nd

Winner walks into the Final.

Eliminator

3rd vs 4th

Winner plays Qualifier 2.

Qualifier 2

Loser Q1 vs Eliminator winner

Last ticket to the Final.

Final

Winner Q1 vs winner Q2

Crowned RPL Season 8 Champion.
